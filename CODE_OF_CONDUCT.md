# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Workshop Planning Agent is an open-source learning project. Everyone should be
able to participate in a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free
environment, regardless of experience, background, identity, or role.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive in discussions, reviews, and issue reports.
- Welcome questions and explain reasoning rather than dismissing people.
- Give credit for ideas, code, documentation, and other contributions.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's personal information.
- Accept feedback gracefully and focus disagreements on the work.
- Follow the event organizer's instructions and applicable laws.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Offensive, sexualized, or hateful language or imagery.
- Plagiarism, misrepresentation, or deliberate falsification of work.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Spamming, disruption, or sustained bad-faith participation.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
documentation, demonstrations, and other project-related communication. The
Hacktoberfest Hack Day - NIAT-ADYPU organizer's Code of Conduct also applies to
event activities.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a concern privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub. Do not
use public issue threads to disclose personal or sensitive details about an
incident. The maintainer may remove content, close contributions, or restrict
participation when this policy is violated. Serious concerns may also be
reported to the event organizers or GitHub.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented where appropriate,
and made without retaliation. Good-faith reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical.
